Quality & safety

Built to a high standard, checked at every step

Every drop of Ponni Drops goes through the same controlled process before it is released for sale.

  1. 01

    Source & pre-filter

    Raw water is drawn and passed through sand and activated-carbon filters to remove sediment, odour and chlorine.

  2. 02

    Reverse osmosis

    RO membranes remove dissolved impurities, then the mineral balance is set for clean, pleasant taste.

  3. 03

    UV & ozonation

    Ultraviolet treatment and ozone disinfection keep the water safe all the way to the bottle.

  4. 04

    Hygienic filling

    Bottles and cans are rinsed, filled and capped on our filling line in a clean, controlled area.

  5. 05

    Seal & batch code

    Every pack gets a tamper-evident cap and a batch code, so each bottle can be traced back to its test.

  6. 06

    Lab release

    Samples from each batch are checked in our lab. Only batches that pass are sent out.

🧪 What our lab checks

  • pH balance
  • TDS (dissolved solids)
  • Turbidity & clarity
  • Taste & odour
  • Microbiology (coliform, E. coli)
  • Chlorides & hardness
  • Seal & leak test
  • Fill volume
